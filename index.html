Engagement Rate Calculator

Enter your likes, comments, shares and followers.

How to use this calculator

Enter the public statistics for a TikTok post or account. The calculator adds likes, comments and shares, then divides the total by your follower count.

Formula: (Likes + Comments + Shares) ÷ Followers × 100.

This is an estimate. Engagement rates vary by account, audience and post.
