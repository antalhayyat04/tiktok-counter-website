What is TikTok Engagement Rate?

TikTok Engagement Rate shows how much your audience interacts with your content. Formula: (Likes + Comments + Shares / Followers) * 100. 3% to 6% is good. Above 6% is viral. This tool by Antal Hayyat from Faisalabad helps Pakistani creators check performance without login.

How to Increase Engagement?

1. Post at 7 PM to 11 PM Pakistan Time
2. Use 3-4 trending hashtags
3. Reply to comments in first 30 mins
4. Make original content.
